# raytraced_minecraft
this is raytraced minecraft! a game that i made in scratch.
----------------------------------------------------------
to play:
keys: W A S D to move and mouse to look around, space to jump, hold to break blocks and tap to put blocks.
click C for creative mode. press R to toggle between raytraced and normal view. press V to toggle information.
click shift and 1 to save and shift and 2 to paste!
-----------------------------------------------------------
you can change the resolution in the project's green flag set varable section.
have fun!
